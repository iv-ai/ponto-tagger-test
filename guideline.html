Before you tag

Image Requirements

The goal: find a "Good Quality Image of the location on a different day."

For every image pair, ask yourself:

1

Is the output image different enough from the input? (not just the same photo with minor tweaks — people, cars, sky, etc.)

2

Does it contain any weird text, signage, or objects that shouldn't be there?

3

Does the output image look natural? (not an empty bar, floating furniture, impossible scene, etc.)

4

Are there any other issues? (bad crop, wiped-out building, wrong environment)

Case 4

Needs New Input

New Input

The input photo is unusable — the AI can't do its job when the source isn't a proper storefront photo.

Flag the input when it shows:

  • Food or drink (not the exterior)
  • A person or portrait
  • Interior of the place (not the facade)
  • An irrelevant or unrelated scene
  • Anything that isn't clearly the outside of the location

Case 5

Flag

Flag

The location is a skip category — we don't process these types of places.

Flag these location types

Cemetery — memorial / burial ground
City Block — apartment / office blocks
Cadastral — land surveying / mapping
Adult Entertainment — clubs, gambling
Event Spaces — banquet halls, convention centers
Lottery Booth — retail / gaming
Professional Place — schools (K-12), government, military, dance/martial arts/acting studios, libraries
Medical Center — dentist, hospital, clinic
Spiritual Center — religious / worship
Residence — houses, condos, apartments
Auto Service — repairs, oil changes, inspections
Banking & Finance — banks, ATMs, financial advisors
Building & Construction — contractors, architects
Commercial Services — printing, repair, consulting, catering, logistics, studios
Manufacture / Factory
Pharmacy — flag + comment "pharmacy"
Closed locations — permanently or temporarily closed
Vehicles as locations — boat, bus, van, ship
When unsure: Flag it and leave a comment explaining why. It's better to flag and explain than to guess.
